What This Is

This dashboard monitors real-time social sentiment from Stocktwits to detect momentum shifts in small-cap and OTC stocks. It combines crowd psychology from social media with AI financial NLP and official regulatory disclosures to give a complete picture of market activity around any screened ticker.

How Data Flows
1
Finviz Elite Screener
Provides the live ticker list. The screener filters for high relative volume and momentum — only stocks with unusual activity appear.
→
2
Pipeline
Fetches the 30 most recent Stocktwits messages per ticker every 90 seconds. Click "Start Pipeline" to run it.
→
3
FinBERT AI Scoring
Each message is passed through FinBERT, a financial NLP model trained on analyst reports and earnings calls, which scores sentiment as bullish or bearish.
→
4
MongoDB
Stores all scored messages. The dashboard queries this in real time.
→
5
Dashboard
Tickers are ranked by composite score, with charts, news, and event tags updating live.
Score System

Every Stocktwits message produces three scores that are combined into one ranking signal.

Sentiment
Raw FinBERT output — how bullish or bearish the message language is. Range: −1 to +1. Positive means bullish.
Trust
Weighted by the poster's follower count, number of ideas published, and account classification. Higher-credibility posters are weighted more.
Impact
Weighted by whether the message contains an event keyword (FDA, earnings, merger, etc.) and engagement signals like likes and replies.
Composite
Combined weighted average of Sentiment × Trust × Impact across all messages in the selected time window. This is the primary ranking column in the main table.
Event Tags — Extracted From Stocktwits Messages

Event tags are automatically detected from the text of Stocktwits messages. When a user writes "$BCRX FDA approval imminent", the system finds keywords and classifies the message with an event tag. These tags appear as colored markers on the score chart inside the detail popup, so you can see exactly what the crowd was reacting to and when.

FDA Approval / Rejection / PDUFA Earnings Beat / Miss Merger / Acquisition Clinical Trial Results Short Squeeze
News View — Confirming the Sentiment

Switch to News View to see all screener tickers as cards, each with a mini price chart, current composite score, recent Stocktwits messages, and recent news articles. The news articles are not the source of the sentiment score — they exist to confirm what Stocktwits users are already talking about. If the crowd is bullish and a legitimate press release just went out, that agreement between social sentiment and official news gives you a more complete picture of what is driving activity around a ticker.

All news sources listed below have an editorial or regulatory standard they are required to follow, which is why they were chosen over general financial media.

PR Newswire
A regulated paid wire distribution service. Companies must verify their identity and take legal responsibility for the accuracy of press releases they distribute here. Fetched via a Google News search scoped to prnewswire.com.
Business Wire
Same category as PR Newswire — a Berkshire Hathaway-owned paid press release distribution service used by publicly traded companies for official announcements. Also fetched via Google News.
GlobeNewswire
Nasdaq's official wire service for press releases. Companies listed on Nasdaq often use GlobeNewswire as their primary distribution channel. Fetched via direct RSS by ticker keyword.
Benzinga
Professional financial news outlet covering analyst ratings upgrades/downgrades, earnings previews, and market-moving stories. Fetched via the Finnhub API — only Benzinga articles are shown, not tabloid sources.
FDA
FDA-related news items detected from Finviz Stocks News. Shows upcoming PDUFA decision dates and approval or rejection events for biotech and pharma tickers.
SEC 8-K
Legally required SEC filings for material events. When a public company issues any significant press release, they are required by law to file an 8-K within four business days. This is the most authoritative confirmation source because it carries SEC oversight. Fetched directly from EDGAR with no date cutoff.
Main Table

Every ticker returned by your Finviz screener appears here, ranked by composite sentiment score descending. Tickers that have received no Stocktwits messages in the current time window collapse into a "No Recent Messages" section at the bottom. Click any ticker row to open the detail popup, which shows a full price chart with event markers, the score history chart, message density over time, and all raw messages from the window.

Sorting — Click any column header to re-sort the table by that column. Click again to reverse the sort direction. You can sort by composite score, price change, message count, market cap, or any other visible column.

Thresholds (Filter Bar) — The filter bar below the metrics row lets you narrow the table by minimum average volume, price change direction (up only / down only), and minimum message count. Adjust these to focus on only the tickers meeting your criteria. Filters apply instantly without reloading data.

Rolling Window — The time window buttons in the top bar (1m · 3m · 5m · 15m · 30m · 1h · 4h · 1d · 1w · 1mo) control how far back messages are included in all score calculations. A 1h window scores only messages from the last hour; a 1d window covers the full trading day. Shorter windows react faster to breaking news; longer windows smooth out noise. The price chart range on every ticker card in News View also matches the selected window.

Live Stocktwits Messages

Click the Signals button in the top bar to open the side panel. It streams the most recent Stocktwits messages in real time across all screener tickers — individual posts that received the highest composite scores. Use it to quickly read what users are saying about any ticker without opening each detail popup.

PDUFA & Earnings Catalysts

The dashboard tracks upcoming FDA decision deadlines (PDUFA dates) from the BioPharma Catalyst database and upcoming earnings dates from Finviz. These appear as countdown labels on ticker cards in the News View. Catalysts often explain sudden spikes in Stocktwits activity days or weeks before the official announcement hits the news wire.

Time Window

The window buttons in the top bar (1m · 3m · 5m · 15m · 30m · 1h · 4h · 1d · 1w · 1mo) control both the price chart range on ticker cards and the sentiment scoring window. Shorter windows surface fast-moving momentum; longer windows show sustained sentiment trends. The composite score, message count, and all chart data update instantly when you change the window.

Screener Performance & Recommended Filters

The number of tickers your Finviz screener returns directly controls how fast the pipeline runs. The pipeline fetches Stocktwits messages for every ticker in parallel (5 workers), then loops. With too many tickers, the loop takes several minutes and sentiment data becomes stale before the next cycle completes.

50–150 tickers
Loop time ~15–45 seconds. Near real-time sentiment updates. Best range for active monitoring.
200–500 tickers
Loop time 1–3 minutes. Sentiment updates are slightly delayed but still useful for research.
500+ tickers
Loop time 3–10+ minutes. Data grows stale between cycles. Consider tightening screener filters.

Our recommended starting filters — set these in the Update Filters modal for a focused, fast screener that catches high-momentum names:

Change: Up only
Limits the universe to stocks moving upward on the day. Focuses the dashboard on tickers where positive sentiment is most relevant to observe.
Rel Vol ≥ 3
Relative volume of 3x means a stock is trading at triple its normal rate — a strong indicator that unusual activity is occurring. Values below 2x let in too many tickers with routine activity. 5x or higher narrows to only the most active names.
Cur Vol ≥ 500k
Ensures there is enough real market participation. Tickers below 500k daily volume often have thin Stocktwits activity and less reliable sentiment data.
Price ≥ $1.00
Filters out sub-penny stocks, which tend to have artificially inflated Stocktwits activity that does not reflect genuine market sentiment.

These four filters together typically return 30–100 tickers, which keeps loop time under 30 seconds and produces a focused dashboard. Adjust rel vol up or down to widen or narrow the universe as needed.
